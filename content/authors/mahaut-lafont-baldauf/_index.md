Mahaut Lafont-Baldauf is a PhD student in the Brain, Behavior, and Learning Lab, co-supervised by Jérôme Prado and Cléa Girard. His doctoral research is part of the PREMATH project, which investigates how children's everyday home environment shapes the development of early numerical and language skills, comparing children born preterm with children born at term.
